# License

This repository collects shaders by many authors. Each shader keeps the license its author gave it; the
collection adds none and grants no rights of its own.

- Shaders from [Shadertoy](https://www.shadertoy.com) fall under Shadertoy's default license,
  [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/), unless the shader states another.
- Shaders from [compute.toys](https://compute.toys) carry no default license: unless the shader states one, all rights
  stay with its author. A compute.toys port of a Shadertoy shader also keeps the Shadertoy original's license.
- A file's header names its source; the source page is the authority on its license.

Non-commercial use only, unless the shader's own license allows more. To reuse a shader beyond its license, ask its
author.
